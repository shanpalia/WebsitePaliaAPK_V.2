This ZIP keeps the existing Supabase/search/updates project and adds:
- The same supplied Android header artwork to the left of PaliaAPK HUB.
- Native app-owned APK downloading for the Android build.
- The Render URL is used only as the source URL; the WebView does NOT navigate to it.
- A PaliaAPK HUB in-app download modal shows MB and percentage.
- The APK is saved under the app's own external-files/PaliaAPK-HUB directory.
- After completion Android's normal APK installer confirmation opens.
- Website (non-native) fallback remains a normal download link.
