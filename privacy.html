Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit PaliaAPK HUB or use the Online Status Android application developed by ShanPalia.

Online Status — contacts

When you choose a contact using the Android system contact picker, the app receives the selected contact's displayed name and phone number so it can show that contact in your list. The app does not request the Android READ_CONTACTS permission or read your entire address book. Selected contact entries are stored locally on your device using app preferences. They are not currently uploaded by the app to a PaliaAPK HUB server.

Status information and notifications

Live online or last-seen status monitoring is not currently connected to an authorized status provider. The app therefore does not currently retrieve or report live status for your selected contacts. Notification permission may be requested on Android 13 and later; allowing it does not itself enable live monitoring. Notification or alert settings in the app may be limited until the related feature is implemented.

Truecaller and other third-party sign-in

The app may offer a third-party sign-in or identity SDK only after it has been integrated and configured. When such a feature is enabled, the relevant provider may process information according to its own privacy policy and the permissions and consent shown during sign-in. This policy will be updated with the specific data fields and purposes before that feature is released. The current app does not claim to use Truecaller to obtain contacts' live online status.

Website technical information

Our hosting provider and website infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time in routine server logs for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

Cookies, advertising, and external links

The website may use cookies or load advertising and other third-party resources. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. When you follow an external link or download link, you leave our website and the external provider's privacy practices apply.

Data retention and control

Contact entries saved by the current app remain in the app's local storage until you remove them in the app or clear its data/uninstall it. Website server logs are retained according to the hosting provider's operational and security practices. You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the website.

Children and security

The services are not designed to encourage children to share personal information. We take reasonable steps appropriate to the services, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. For privacy questions, use the PaliaAPK HUB Contact page.
